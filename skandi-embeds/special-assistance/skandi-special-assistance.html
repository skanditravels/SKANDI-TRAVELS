Overall information

Assistance can span every handoff.

Use this page as a planning hub, then confirm the exact service with the relevant operating provider. What is available can vary by airport, airline, property, vehicle and destination.

01 · AIRPORT

Wheelchair & mobility

Airport assistance may include help through the terminal, security, boarding and arrival. If you use your own wheelchair or mobility aid, confirm handling requirements and any battery restrictions with the operating airline.

02 · FLIGHT

Medical & onboard needs

Medical equipment, oxygen, seating restrictions and service-animal procedures can depend on the operating carrier and route. Advance approval or documentation may be required for some services.

03 · STAY

Accessible accommodation

Accessibility is property-specific. Check any stored information about step-free access, lifts, adapted rooms and accessible facilities, then confirm critical requirements directly with the hotel.

04 · GROUND

Transfers & experiences

Vehicle access, wheelchair storage and accessible meeting points vary. Review the provider information available for transfers, tours and activities before departure.

Common questions

Before requesting assistance.

When should I arrange airport or airline assistance?
Arrange it as early as you can and follow the operating airline's instructions. Some aviation assistance services use an advance-notice window of up to 48 hours, while other requests can have different deadlines.
Should I check both the airline and the airport?
Yes. The operating airline and airport can each be responsible for different parts of the journey. Use the published information above for both, and confirm any critical requirement directly.
What if no accessibility information is shown for my hotel or transfer?
It means SKANDI does not currently have a published assistance-related field for that item in the canonical data returned to this page. Do not assume that a service is or is not accessible; contact the provider or SKANDI Support before travel.
Can I travel with medication or medical equipment?
Requirements vary by equipment, carrier, security rules and destination. Keep essential medication accessible during travel and confirm documentation, battery, oxygen and equipment rules with the relevant provider before departure.
