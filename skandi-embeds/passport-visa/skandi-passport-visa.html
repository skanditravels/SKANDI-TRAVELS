Passport guidance

The details that commonly change the answer

These are planning reminders only. The exact rule comes from the destination, transit authority and operating carrier requirements for your trip.

Passport validity

There is no universal six-month rule. Required validity can differ by destination, nationality and travel document, including whether the passport must remain valid beyond the planned stay.

Transit points

A connection can introduce separate document or visa requirements even when the final destination would otherwise be permitted. Check every country and airport in the itinerary.

Children & guardians

Children need documents accepted for their nationality and route. Some journeys can also require consent, custody or relationship documentation when a minor travels with one parent or another adult.

!

Damaged, emergency or temporary passports

Acceptance varies by country and carrier. A document that is damaged, provisional, emergency-issued or otherwise non-standard should be verified specifically rather than assumed valid.

Visas & electronic authorization

A passport alone may not be enough.

Depending on nationality and itinerary, a traveler may need a visa, visa waiver, electronic travel authorization or other pre-departure approval. SKANDI can show connected guidance, but final eligibility remains with the relevant authority and operating carrier.

Common questions

Passport & visa FAQ

Do transit countries have separate requirements?
They can. Transit requirements depend on nationality, airport, document type, whether border control is crossed and the rules in force on the date of travel. Every transit country should be checked.
I have dual citizenship. Which passport should I use?
Use a passport that satisfies the route's requirements and ensure the booking details match the document presented for travel. Some countries require their citizens to enter or leave using that country's passport.
What if the connected checker has no detailed result?
Treat that as a signal to verify the trip directly with official government, border-control and visa-authority sources. A missing provider field is not confirmation that no requirement exists.
What happens if my documents do not meet the requirements?
The operating carrier or border authority can refuse travel when required documents or authorizations are missing or invalid. Any resulting cost is governed by the applicable booking, supplier and authority rules.
