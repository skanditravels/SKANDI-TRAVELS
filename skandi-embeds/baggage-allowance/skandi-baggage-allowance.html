BAGGAGE BASICS

Know what you are packing into.

These are general planning principles, not a universal allowance. Exact piece counts, dimensions and weights come from the operating airline and your booked fare.

01 / Personal item

Under-seat item

Some fares include only a small personal item. It normally needs to fit beneath the seat and can have a different size limit from an overhead cabin bag.

02 / Cabin baggage

Overhead bag

Cabin-bag inclusion, dimensions and weight vary by carrier and fare. A compliant bag can still be gate-checked when aircraft or operational conditions require it.

03 / Checked baggage

Hold luggage

Checked allowance can be included, optional or unavailable. Piece and weight concepts differ between airlines, routes and ticket products.

04 / Special baggage

Plan it early

Sports equipment, musical instruments, mobility equipment, child equipment and other special items can require advance notification or separate handling.

SPECIAL ITEMS & SECURITY

Items that need a second check.

Rules below are planning reminders. The operating airline, airport security authority and local regulations can impose more specific requirements.

CHILD EQUIPMENT

Strollers & car seats

Allowance and aircraft-door delivery differ by carrier and airport. Check whether equipment must be tagged or checked at a counter.

SPORTS / OVERSIZE

Reserve space

Bicycles, skis, golf bags and other oversized items can require advance booking because hold space and handling are limited.

BATTERIES

Powerbanks & lithium

Portable lithium batteries are commonly subject to cabin-baggage restrictions and capacity limits. Check the airline's dangerous-goods rules.

SECURITY

Liquids & screening

Airport screening technology and liquid rules vary. Check departure and transfer airports instead of assuming one rule applies throughout the journey.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you close the suitcase.

Can travelers on the same booking pool baggage weight?
Some airlines allow pooling and others do not. Individual-bag safety limits can still apply. Check the operating airline and fare conditions.
Is baggage included for infants?
Infant allowances vary by airline, route and ticket type. Strollers, car seats and other child equipment can have separate rules.
What if my cabin bag is too large or heavy?
The airline may require the bag to be checked and may charge an airport or gate fee. Handling depends on the carrier, route, fare and airport.
Should I rely on the packing list for weather?
No. The packing tool estimates a season from date and hemisphere and applies your selected expected conditions. Check the destination forecast close to departure.
